Python Backend Developer - AI Platform

About the Role
We are building an AI-powered hiring platform and need a backend developer who can turn language models and search into reliable, well-tested APIs.

Responsibilities
• Design, build and maintain REST APIs and microservices for our AI products
• Build NLP pipelines that analyse and rank unstructured text such as resumes and job descriptions
• Model data and write efficient queries against relational databases
• Containerise services and ship them through automated CI/CD pipelines
• Write unit tests and review code with the team

Requirements
• 3+ years of professional experience in Python backend development
• Strong experience with FastAPI, Flask or Django
• Solid SQL skills and hands-on experience with PostgreSQL
• Practical experience with NLP and machine learning
• Experience with Docker
• Experience building REST APIs
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field

Nice to have
• AWS or another cloud platform
• Kubernetes
• Experience with LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
• Experience with vector search
